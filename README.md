## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🚗 Passo: Driving-Lesson Booking Platform
Connects pupils with driving instructors through dedicated roles.
- Real-time lesson requests where instructors can accept or send counter-offers instantly (**Laravel Reverb**)
- **Stripe Connect** payments: the platform keeps its fee and automatically releases the balance to the instructor once a booking is completed

`Laravel` `Laravel Reverb` `Stripe Connect` `MySQL`

### 🤖 PolyCareers: AI-Powered Job Board
Job platform for Poly Tech Talent, combining curated recruiter listings with open roles from across the market.
- AI resume tool: improve an existing resume, tailor it to a job posting, or generate one from scratch
- Location-based job search, detailed listing pages and an apply workflow
- Account and session management for candidates

### 🎪 Logilux: Event Agency Platform (Rome)
Corporate website and tooling for an event management and hostess/promoter agency serving trade fairs, museums and corporate events.
- Web and mobile **event accreditation system** where guests register and upload data/images ahead of events
- "Join the Team" portal, job listings, client login and an automated quote-request system

### 🚚 LoadSecure: Postal Order & Driver Tracking
- Create Postal Orders and driver records, then assign orders to drivers
- **Real-time driver location tracking** and delivery breadcrumbs on completion
